# Expose launch film — sketch-first concept v4

**7 November 2026, B058 revises B057. Discussion draft only. No v4 video, generated imagery, voice or editable timeline has been made.** The user wants the opening to feel like a stand-alone, surprising ad sketch. The product appears as the answer to that sketch's problem, around 12 seconds, then a narrator carries a clear Expose story.

## Recommended cold open: The Best Friend Awards

The film appears to be a very serious awards ceremony at 2 AM. Riya accepts a trophy for knowing the group better than anyone. Manoj asks one tiny question; her speech collapses. The award envelope opens into an Expose case file. The move is a genre change with a reason: a boast needs evidence. This is an original premise built for Expose, using the comedic **setup → interruption → pivot → payoff** structure the user described. It does not reproduce a particular creator's lines or campaign. Riya remains the film's subject: the captured Spotlight guess, Bluff vote, −50 result and Friend Card all come from her demo view.

Alternate sketches if the ceremony feels too theatrical:

1. **Emergency press conference:** Manoj announces he knows every friend. Riya asks for one fact; reporters' microphones become answer cards.
2. **Breaking news bulletin:** A dramatic report announces an epidemic of overconfident best friends; its evidence board becomes the Expose dossier.

The award version has the cleanest visual handoff: **envelope → case file → true app card**. Its marigold stage spotlight is already in Expose's palette, and its trophy/avatars can be animated as layered assets instead of using static screen captures.
