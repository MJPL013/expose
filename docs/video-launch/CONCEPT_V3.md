# Expose launch film — concept v3 for discussion

**7 November 2026, B057 revises B055 and B056. Superseded for discussion by the [sketch-first v4 concept](CONCEPT_V4.md). Storyboard and provisional script only. No v3 animation or MP4 has been made.** The user rejected the screenshot-based first cut and silent v2 animatic. This concept uses the current localhost app as product evidence and the app's own case-file identity as the animated world.

## The idea

**One sentence:** A late-night challenge between friends opens a case file; Expose turns their confidence into guesses, bluffs, a verified answer and a personal verdict.

**Audience:** Hackathon judges and potential players seeing the product for the first time. The film must show who plays (2–7 friends), where (together or remote), what they do (rate, guess, bluff, vote), the payoff (verified answer and points) and what they take home (an optional Friend Card).

**Voice shape:** Two friends speak naturally in the first six seconds. A single, warm and slightly mischievous narrator carries the rest. Their voices are film performances supplied by the user through Gnani TTS, not game audio. Hindi/Hinglish is kept in the opening; the narration is English for a clear hackathon pitch. The narrator describes consequences and actions, not a feature menu.

**Visual rule:** The animated world is built from Expose's real visual grammar: charcoal room, warm cream dossier, marigold controls, red stamp, avatars, fingerprint and the original lockup. A generated layered late-night setting can supply atmosphere; it must contain no generated app UI, words or logo. Real screen recordings are short **evidence inserts**, under about 12 seconds across the whole film, only when an actual product action matters. The dossier card, answer card and Friend Card are shared shapes that carry the viewer between animated scenes and true UI. No alternating PPT/screenshot panels and no repeated in/out camera move.

The user-linked [Opus 5.5 video collection](https://github.com/yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos) is a prompt/reference gallery, not a production tool. The [Food Craving Product Launch Film prompt](https://github.com/yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos/blob/main/prompts/ultimaxbt-549714.md) gives a useful continuity principle: objects transform into the next scene, with a planned camera move and a clear hero beat. The [UI Motion Loop prompt](https://github.com/yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos/blob/main/prompts/flxrnc-251921.md) is useful for state morphing, cursor contact and defined settling. The [SaaS Launch prompt](https://github.com/yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos/blob/main/prompts/screeendev-981790.md) calls for typing and genuine app interaction. These are direction cues, not a request to copy their visuals. The linked prompts were read; their entire video gallery was not reviewed.

The opening setting, card world and avatar tokens need **actual layer animation**: cards hinge, tokens travel, marks draw, selected answers change state, the folder turns and the stamp strikes. Generated art is limited to coherent environment/material layers; the product interactions use browser footage and original UI assets. Design key frames should be approved before any 60-second build.

## Decisions to settle in discussion

1. **Visual world:** recommended layered illustrated hostel desk and case file, using generated atmosphere plus app-native objects. An alternative is a fully graphic dossier world with no physical desk; this would feel cleaner and less character-led.
2. **Narrator language:** recommended English narrator after the Hinglish opening for immediate judge comprehension. A bilingual narrator can work if the final judging audience is mainly Hindi-speaking; subtitles remain either way.
3. **AI host:** recommended as a short optional proof beat only when actual current output can be captured. The main story works without it.

After these are settled: make three **distinct full-size style frames** (opening, Bluff impact, end), choose one, record the narrator/friend voices, then animate and edit around measured speech. Keep the source screenshots as visual reference and proof, never as the full film's continuous background.

**Editing handoff:** Huashu's current studio is useful for scene preview and timing values, but it is not the requested clip/audio-track editor. Build the animated shots as replaceable motion segments, then assemble video, dialogue, captions, music and effects in an editable multitrack project suited to the user's editor (for example, Resolve XML or a native Kdenlive project) alongside the MP4. The exact handoff format should be chosen before production.
