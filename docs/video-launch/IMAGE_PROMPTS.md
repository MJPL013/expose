# Expose launch film: image generation prompts

**B054, 7 November 2026. Prompt pack only. No image was generated or selected.**

## Asset strategy

Create one landscape master still for the first five seconds and a later crop behind the genuine Friend Card. Animation comes from a slow camera move and carefully masked depth on that same approved image. The main game sequence uses current Expose browser capture. The actual Expose logo, typography, UI, avatars, questions, scores, stamps and Friend Card are supplied from the project or current app, never generated by the image model.

Use these as **style references only**, not edit targets: [current home](../../reference-images/live-2026-11-07/01-home-phone.png) for palette and identity; [current Friend Card](../../reference-images/live-2026-11-07/10-friend-card.png) for cream paper and red stamp treatment. Do not use the older black-and-white concept images. The references are local and ignored by Git.

## Copy-ready master still prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: single landscape 16:9 editorial still for a premium 60-second Expose product-launch film; it will be cropped slightly and animated in post.
Input images: Image 1, current Expose home screenshot, style and colour reference only. Image 2, current Expose Friend Card screenshot, paper/material reference only. Neither image is an edit target. Do not recreate their UI, logo, text, avatars or card content.
Primary request: A candid late-night friend-group moment at a common-room tabletop. Three young adult South Asian friends are suggested only by naturally posed hands and partial sleeves at the edge of frame; no faces. An unlit black phone and one clean cream paper dossier are on a dark tabletop. Their placement implies that someone is about to challenge the group. Keep it human, playful and premium, with a real physical environment rather than a fantasy detective set.
Scene/backdrop: A simple hostel or shared-apartment table at night, uncluttered and believable. No extra props beyond the phone and dossier. The phone screen is off and contains no interface.
Style/medium: Photorealistic editorial photography, controlled 45-degree overhead view, natural hand anatomy, tactile but clean paper grain, realistic fabric and surface texture. One coherent camera and one coherent light source.
Composition/framing: Wide 16:9. Put the phone and cream dossier in the middle area large enough to read as objects. Leave a clean dark upper-left area for the real title to be typeset later. Leave crop safety around every hand and paper edge for a gentle 3% camera push. Keep the paper fairly planar so the real Friend Card can later be composited over a crop without extreme perspective distortion.
Lighting/mood: Warm, restrained marigold practical light on the paper edges, soft neutral fill, plausible contact shadows. It should feel like 2 AM with friends, lively but not loud. No coloured haze, lens flare or glow.
Colour palette: Expose charcoal #0e0d12 and #17161d, cream #f4efe6, a small marigold #ffb020 accent, and at most a tiny red #ff3b3b accent. Keep full colour and natural skin tones; do not make the scene monochrome.
Text: none. Leave all words, symbols and branding to the editor using the real Expose assets.
Constraints: exactly three people's hands implied, believable number and anatomy of fingers, one phone, one dossier, no invented product features. Maintain a clean read at 1920x1080 after a small crop. This is a background plate, not an app screenshot.
Avoid: gradients, neon, cyberpunk, metallic chrome, purple/blue glow, particles, smoke, generic stock-photo smiles, dramatic crime-scene tape, magnifying glasses, handcuffs, fake UI, visible app text, watermarks, extra fingers, distorted hands, black-and-white treatment.
```

## Acceptance before animation

1. Compare the result beside the current home and Friend Card screenshots at the same size. Reject any new palette, cyberpunk styling, fake interface, unreadable silhouette or implausible hand anatomy.
2. Check the upper-left dark area can hold the hook in Bricolage Grotesque at full readable size. Check the cream paper can support a later genuine Friend Card insert without perspective distortion.
3. Test two crops of **the same image**: an opening crop around phone/hands and an ending crop around dossier/card. The image should remain recognizably one location.
4. Mask only existing table, hands and paper for a maximum 3% camera push and a slight paper-edge separation. Keep shadows aligned; if a mask makes the still look like cardboard, keep the still whole and use just the camera move.

The built-in image-generation tool is unavailable in this session. The imagegen skill's CLI fallback needs an API key and is used only if the user explicitly chooses that route; no fallback has been run.
