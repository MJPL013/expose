# Expose: 60-second launch film plan

**B052, revised by B053 and B054 on 7 November 2026. A first video draft was rendered as B055; [draft details and corrected dialogue](video-launch/DRAFT_01.md).** This page preserves the original treatment and planned image-generated tabletop direction.

## Product read

Expose is a multiplayer party-game PWA for 2–7 real friends. A room owner chooses Spotlight or Bluff, a setting such as Hostel 2 AM or Goa Trip, and whether the group is together or remote. Friends join a room, rate themselves and one another, answer or vote through scored rounds, and finish with a verdict and an individual Friend Card that each player may approve or keep private. An optional AI host is implemented to react at checkpoints and remember callbacks within the same room. [Current game flow](GAME_FLOW.md), [interactive host](INTERACTIVE_HOST.md), and [current status](STATUS.md) are the source of these claims.

**What the film should leave in a judge's head:** “This turns a group of friends into a funny, competitive case file. I understand how to enter, what I do, what makes the reveal satisfying, and what I take away.” This is a product launch with a miniature story, rather than a feature list.

**Core idea:** **The case opens tonight.** We overhear three friends teasing each other into a game. Their banter drives the cut; the app UI quietly shows how the challenge works. Bluff is the primary loop because its anonymous vote and verified answer read immediately on screen. Spotlight gets a short, clear visual beat so neither mode is hidden. The performance should feel like friends on a late-night call, with a confident product-launch finish rather than a tutorial voiceover.

**Subtitle treatment:** Keep the original spoken words in the audio; use concise English subtitles for code-switched lines (for example, “You know me, right?”, “Send the link, quick!”, “C. Definitely.”). Keep the captions away from the app's action buttons. Perform the Hindi/Hinglish lightly, as everyday speech. The reveal gets the loudest reaction; the surrounding lines should feel conversational.

**Performance rhythm:** Let “I'm in” overlap the last avatar joining and let Riya's “Haan. Sure.” arrive a fraction late, like a friend reacting rather than an announcer reading a cue. Keep the vote pause genuinely quiet, then allow Ananya's laugh to run under the stamped reveal. The end-tag voice alone has a polished launch cadence.

## Visual and sound direction

- Lock the look to the current app: charcoal `#0e0d12` and `#17161d`, cream paper `#f4efe6`, marigold `#ffb020`, and red stamp `#ff3b3b`. Use its actual Expose lockup, dossier cards, avatars, Bricolage Grotesque/DM Sans/Anton type roles and screen captures. Generated imagery supplies a physical scene, never a substitute logo, interface, score or Friend Card.
- Make **one** image-generated master still of a late-night tabletop. Use it for the opening and return to a different crop behind the final card, so the image becomes a visual location rather than a sequence of unrelated AI pictures. [The exact prompt and acceptance criteria](video-launch/IMAGE_PROMPTS.md) are ready; no generated asset exists yet.
- Give the still limited, credible motion: separate its existing table, hands and paper into masks; move the camera about 3% over the opening, shift the dossier edge only enough to reveal depth, and retain its light direction and shadows. Keep hands still unless real footage supplies their motion. When the audience reads a question, vote or card, hold the frame steady.
- Cut on **actions**, not on a library of transitions: a paper edge reveals the actual home case file; the joining avatar leads into the roster; a selected answer leads into the anonymous vote; 0.6 seconds of quiet precede the hard-cut **VERIFIED** reveal. Let the real app's stamp provide the one strong impact. The Friend Card returns to the opening tabletop. The logo closes on flat app charcoal, with no added gradient.
- Music: playful late-night detective pulse with room for pauses. Use licensed or original music and isolated UI effects. The half-beat silence before **VERIFIED** is the main sound moment. Record the cast as film dialogue, clean and close to the microphone; do not add synthetic voice-chat indicators to the app UI. No private answer, room invitation or provider output enters the film without deliberate approval.
- Keep a clear hierarchy: one statement at a time, usually under seven words on screen. Use the real UI for interaction and editorial text only for the story's connective tissue.

**Edit exclusions:** no extra colour gradients, neon, chrome, floating particles, random wipes, repeated zooms, fake app controls, image-generated words, or black-and-white concept photos. A move must direct attention to a new fact or land a reaction. The existing app UI may retain its own subtle lighting; the film adds no new background look over it.

The [timed edit data](video-launch/timeline.json) names the ten beats and the silence before the hero reveal. [Claim sources](video-launch/FACTS.md) keep the on-screen 2–7 friends and mode labels tied to code. The installed Video Director timeline validator checks this draft; dialogue timings remain estimated until the voices are recorded.

## Production route after this plan is approved

1. Capture clean current interactions with fictional identities from localhost. Record online room/join footage only with a disposable test room, mask the temporary invitation, and use a fresh verified host moment if the AI beat remains in the film. Do not portray the demo squad as a physical multiplayer test.
2. Generate and inspect the single tabletop master from the approved prompt when an image-generation model is available; reject drift from the app palette or awkward hands. Build a 1920×1080, 30 fps, 1,800-frame master in Remotion for precise image reframing, masked depth, typography and phone compositing. Use browser captures/recordings for the product screens; Remotion supplies motion design, not fake app behavior. Make a 9:16 version only if the hackathon requires it. [Remotion composition](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/composition), [rendering](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/render), and [Playwright screenshots](https://playwright.dev/docs/screenshots) document this tool fit.
3. Record the three friend performances and the separate end tag, mix the music and stamp effects, render, and review at normal speed on phone and desktop. Check exact 60-second duration, readable subtitles, language pronunciation, caption sync and every product claim against the captured build.

**Open acceptance boundary:** Current docs report browser/mock verification, but no physical two-device match or new live AI/voice acceptance for the latest hands-free revision. The optional host beat must use verified current footage or be replaced by another real gameplay moment. [Current status](STATUS.md).
